## Publication note, 18 August 2026

Two disclosures that sit outside the audit as delivered on 10 August. They are recorded here rather than in the text so that the delivered document and its published hash stay identical.

**How this audit was run.** The claim ledger, the primary-source retrieval and the first-pass grading were produced with AI assistance: language-model agents fetching and reading the underlying filings, transcripts, revenue tables and press articles in their original languages, each verdict recorded against the specific document it rests on. The disconfirmation pass that attacked our own adverse verdicts ran the same way under a separate instruction, and it overturned two of our verdicts, downgraded two more and corrected three errors in our notes. SPEC Research reviewed the output and is responsible for every verdict as published. We state this because the audit asks readers of AI-assisted research to ask who re-verified the sources and against what. That question applies to us. The answer that matters is in the appendix: every verdict names the document it rests on, so a reader can check the record rather than trust the process.

**On the link annotations.** The audit reports 84 external link annotations resolving to two homepages. The geometry, for precision: 80 of the 84 sit in two identical rectangles repeated in the page footer across 40 pages, which is template chrome and carries no implication. Four are placed in the body of the document, and those four resolve to the same two homepages. The finding is the one the audit states, unchanged: the document's only clickable destinations are its publisher's.
